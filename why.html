WHY WE'RE BUILDING C-LEVELS

The argument about kids and AI
is often framed as a tradeoff.

Equip them and risk hollowing them out. Shield them and risk leaving them behind. The fears are valid. What if the worry isn't really about kids?

Most of us — adults included — happily outsource the thinking. We are all forming habits with this tool right now, mostly without noticing. The same tool, asked differently, can challenge, sharpen, stretch thinking instead of replacing it. Used that way, it feels, accurately, like work. It is also the use most worth practising before the easier version becomes default.

Around twelve to fifteen, most young people first meet these tools in their own hands. What they do with them in those years tends to stick. We're working on the container that turns that window into habits worth keeping. The way in, we believe, is intrigue.
