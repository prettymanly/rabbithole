WHY WE’RE BUILDING THIS

The argument about kids and AI is often framed as a tradeoff. Equip them and risk hollowing them out. Shield them and risk leaving them behind. The fears are valid. What if the worry isn’t really about kids?

Most of us — adults included — happily outsource our thinking. We are all forming habits with AI right now, mostly without noticing. The same tool, asked differently, can challenge, sharpen, stretch thinking instead of replacing it. Used that way, it feels, accurately, like work. It is also the use most worth practising before the easier version becomes default.

Kids in Singapore now meet AI at ten, inside the school’s guardrails. By twelve to fifteen, the guardrails fall away. We’re building the container that turns that window into habits worth keeping. The way in, we believe, is intrigue.
